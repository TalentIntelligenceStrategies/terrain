Common questions

Your search and your results

How long does a search take?
A few seconds. The bar at the top of your results says how long the last one took, and holds the words you searched with so you can change them and run it again.
Can Terrain tell me whether I am free to file?
No. Terrain shows you what has already been filed and which company holds it. Whether any of it stands in your way is a legal question, and that one is for a patent attorney.
What does starring a patent do?
Two things. It adds the patent to your starred set, which has a page of its own and is what you can take out of Terrain. And it makes Find similar available: that re-orders the whole result set by how near each patent is to the one you starred, so closer ones surface to the top. No patent enters or leaves the set, and Restore puts the original order back.
Where does the patent data come from?
The Innovue database — around 180 million records, covering the major offices. Terrain searches it and lays out what it returns; it does not hold a database of its own.
Why does a search cover only some countries?
Data coverage on the home screen lists every source and when each was last taken in. Search settings let you narrow to the ones you care about before a run.
Does sorting or filtering run a new search?
No, and neither spends a point. They change the order and what is shown of the set you already have. Pressing Search is what starts a new run.

Points and your plan

When do points come back?
At the start of each quarter. Points you have not spent do not carry over, and a search is charged when you press Search rather than when the results finish arriving.
What spends points?
Running a search. Working with what it returns does not — opening a record, reading its drawings, sorting, filtering, starring and finding similar are all free, however many times you do them.
What happens if I run out?
Everything you have already built stays open and stays readable. You cannot start a new build until the next quarter or until you move up a plan.

Your starred set

Who can see my projects?
Only you. There are no shared workspaces and no seats in Terrain — an account is one person's.
Can I take my results out of Terrain?
Yes — the patents you starred. Their own page has two buttons: a spreadsheet and a Markdown list, both carrying the fields already on your screen. The point is that you can work on the data wherever you already work.
What happens to my projects if I close my account?
They go, along with every search you have run and everything you starred. Nothing is recoverable afterwards, so export what you need first.
