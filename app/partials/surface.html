Where the holders are from

Where the companies are based — which is a different question from where the patents were granted. A rival abroad who has not filed in your market cannot stop you there; one based anywhere who has filed there can.

Where it is filed

A patent only protects the country it is granted in. This is where the patents in your scope were filed — not where the technology is sold, and not the whole database, which covers 100+ countries.

Live and expired

A patent lasts about 20 years, then it expires. An expired patent is published knowledge, not an obstacle — anyone may use it. The bars split each holder’s filings the same way.
