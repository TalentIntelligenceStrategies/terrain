matched Sorting, filtering and grouping change what you are looking at, never what was searched. The set is fixed by your sentence and your search settings; everything in the bar above rearranges it. To search for something else, edit the sentence at the top and search again.

Star a patent that is close to your idea. Your starred patents stay with you while you search, and they are what you can take out of Terrain. Starring also makes Find similar available, which re-orders the set by how near each patent is to what you starred — no patent enters or leaves, only the order changes, and you can put it back.

The figure on the right of each row is the engine's relevance score. It is how close the search put that patent to what you described.

That ordering is a ranking, not a verdict. A patent near the top is close to what you described. It does not mean it affects you, and it does not mean the ones below it do not.
