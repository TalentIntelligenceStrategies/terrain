Before you write

How long does a build take?
A few minutes. You can close Terrain and come back — the map is waiting when you return, and switching a notification on in Account settings will tell you when it is.
When do points come back?
At the start of each quarter. Points you have not spent do not carry over, and a build is charged when you approve it rather than when the map finishes drawing.
Can Terrain tell me whether I am free to file?
No. Terrain shows you what has already been filed and which company holds it. Whether any of it stands in your way is a legal question, and that one is for a patent attorney.
